Why this sheet exists. Most portfolio designs show one screen with ideal data in it. Deciding what a metric shows when there is nothing to show, what a button does while it is working, and how a source that is unavailable appears — that is the difference between a mockup and something a developer can build from without asking questions. These states are all implemented in root-cause.html.
