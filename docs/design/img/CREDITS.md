### Why not the originals

`speed3.avif` and `speed4.jpg` in Downloads are **watermarked stock previews** — Slidesdocs and
Alamy respectively, with the agency name repeated across the image and an ID stamped in the corner.
They cannot be used: the watermark is visible, and publishing them would be copyright infringement.
`hero-dashboard.jpg` is the same subject, properly licensed, and free of any mark.
